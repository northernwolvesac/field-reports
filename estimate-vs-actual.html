Calibration by category

Estimate = the estimate linked to the project on the Bid Board (In progress / Accepted first); hours and material are computed with the same engine as the estimate page (labor factors, waste, group multipliers), optional lines excluded. Payroll $ = Labor Cost W-2 + payroll taxes + 1099 from the Mason journal (Labor Costs page) for the pay dates shown; subs spent = Projects → Costs (installation sub crews are paid through A/P, so with "count subcontractors" on they are part of the actual and the estimate's own subs / services $ join the estimate side); actual hours = actual $ ÷ loaded cost per man-hour (set above). % complete = billed ÷ revised contract from the latest pay application, otherwise the PM % from the Workload Planner. Projected = actual ÷ % complete (only when ≥ 25 % complete); variance = projected − estimate (red = the job costs more than estimated). Jobs whose first payroll is the first journal payroll, or whose start date is before it, carry labor that is not in the journal — they are flagged partial and left out of the calibration unless included; an estimate with fewer than 5 lines or under 20 % of the labor budget is a thin estimate and is never used. Materials spent comes from Projects → Costs (vendor invoices, as of the date shown). Total cost = labor + subs + material on both sides — the safest comparison, because a scope the estimate carries as material or services (controls, insulation, crane) is often paid as a subcontractor and lands in "subs spent". The calibration fits projected hours = Σ factor × estimated hours per category over the comparable jobs (non-negative least squares); a factor of 1.20 means the field needs 20 % more hours than the estimate for that category. Treat it as indicative until there are 10+ comparable jobs.
