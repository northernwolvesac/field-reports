## 2. The current proposed fuel oil system (approved 15.2 product data + latest drawings Rev.02/Rev.03)

**Vendor:** Core Engineered Solutions (package SBM5622 R002, 6/4/26), approved 6/11/26 (NET).

**Storage tank FOT-1** (sub-cellar "Tank/Pump Room"):
- UL 142, **134 gal nominal / 120 gal working**, single-wall rectangular tank inside a **200% open rupture basin**. Core model SWR00134UL-2O-MB-NP.
- Size 54"L x 36"W x 49"H (OAL 4'-6", OAW 3'-0", OAH 4'-1"). Mild carbon steel: inner 12 ga, outer 10 ga, lap weld outside only.

## 3. How it evolved, and what the reviewers kept rejecting

1. **Product data, 15.0 to 15.2:** two rounds whose content and comments could not be read (15.0 and 15.1 files unreadable). Core R002 was then approved "No Exceptions Taken" on 6/11/26. The equipment package itself (tank, skid, Brugg, controls) is **approved**.
2. **Shop drawing 19.0 (FO-SC Rev.00, 6/19):** **Rejected 7/22** after an ACE site visit with the GC: "wait for owner's direction and decision".
   - It had 12 open drafter flags, mostly the 2-hr enclosure being very low (B.5'-2" to B.5'-6") under existing services, an enclosure conflicting with a door and with existing risers, confirmation of fill/vent and supply/return riser locations, the supply pipe at 5'-9" due to slope, and "provide submittals for the generator".
   - The rejection appears tied to the fill/vent location and the routing decision, which led to RFI#26 on 8/20.
3. **Rev.01 (9/15, "as per coordination"):** sub-cellar risers relabelled "TO 2ND FLOOR". Flag 3 revised to "VERY LOW (B.7'-1") DUE TO EXISTING SYSTEMS AND NEW DUCTWORK ABOVE". Flags 6, 8, 9 and 10 were removed.
4. **Rev.02 (9/17, "updated"):** risers labelled "FROM SUB-CELLAR TO ROOF" on every floor, with the 5th-floor route through Bedroom 5D. This was submitted to KBD only on 10/19 as 19.2, limited to the generator supply/return path. It was returned **Approved as Noted** on 10/20 for supply/return **only**.
5. **Rev.03 (10/13, "as per returned drawing"; SUB-032 R3 cover 10/14):** shortened route (FOS 164', FOR 142'). Only two flags remain: enclosure very low at B.4'-11" and at B.5'-3". It **still includes FOF/FOV** at the old location. No review is on file.

In October it carved fill/vent out of the approval ("shall be resubmitted ... coordinated with contractor on new location") and repeated that on flags 1, 4, 5 and 7. ## 6. Risks, blockers, and what is needed next

1. **Fill/vent is unapproved (critical path).** No drawing yet shows the RFI#26 Option 2 garage location.
   - ACE requires the fill/vent to "stick out of the building", at least 2 ft from openings (2 ft above and 2 ft beside the garage door).
   - The Core RFB box is a recess/flush wall box. Confirm it can be mounted so the fill connection is exterior, or change the box type.
   - Also confirm truck access and fill-hose path through the garage, and the vent terminal height and clearances per NYC MC/FC (verify the code distances; ACE gave only 2 ft).
   - The fill box (NWAC #30) and RFI#03 have no response. Submit the box formally to KBD with the fill/vent drawing.
2. **Fill/vent material is not defined** anywhere (only 2" FOF / 4" FOV sizes). The 2" normal vent on the tank vs the 4" FOV on the drawings needs reconciling. Expect the reviewer to ask.
3. **Fire rating / double enclosure:** ACE now wants another 2-hr layer around the supply/return enclosure, and to "avoid penetrating the shaft". This can change scope and cost (who builds it), headroom, and routing through occupied/residential floors (5th floor Bedroom 5D, flagged "out-of-scope area").
4. **Clearances:** enclosures already very low (B.4'-11", B.5'-2" to B.5'-6" in cellar/sub-cellar). ACE requires 3 ft wide x 7 ft high clearance at the supply line, which may force re-routing.
5. **Revision control:** KBD reviewed Rev.02 (9/17). NWAC's Rev.03 (10/13, SUB-032 R3) has not been reviewed and still carries fill/vent. The next resubmittal (19.3) must reconcile Rev.03 with the 10/20 comments. The returned 19.1 is missing from Drive. Edward Arcari's review is still "Pending" on 19.2.
6. **Leak detection:** ACE wants an "oil leak detection probe cable". It is not in the Core package (Core has an LMS vacuum monitor and point leak sensors). Get a Core add or product data, and a price.
7. **Generator tank and structure:** belly tank shown as 134 gal (Core/NWAC) vs 75 gal (Zierke/Discovery cut). Set weight 8,250 lb; ACE wants structural to confirm. Confirm the belly-tank fittings and switch interface (Core expects 4-point level, leak, and vent overflow switches on BT1). Roof FDNY path/clearance per the new architect sketch; roof flashing by NWAC.
8. **FDNY / DOB:** the generator is filed under job M01244603-S1. The fuel oil fill/vent relocation, tank and piping likely need to be reflected in the filing or an amendment (the sibling agent / design side should confirm who files). The Brugg NYC DOB approval data is in 15.2.
9. **Coordination:** Infinity Electric (power 115 V/30 A to the FOP-1 panel, Modbus to BMS, annunciator wiring, level/leak sensor wiring) and Standard Fire Protection (fill box "install above sprinkler connection"; FDC is at the same frontage) received 15.2. Confirm who wires what.
10. **Release:** 15.2 was approved 6/11. Check whether Core was given signed release to fabricate, and the tank/skid delivery status (not in these files).

**Next steps (suggested):**
- Ask KBD/ACE for the architect's FDNY sketch and a clarification on the "another 2-hr layer" requirement.
- Field-verify the garage fill/vent location with the GC.
- Have All-City issue Rev.04: supply/return per the 10/20 comments, plus fill/vent per RFI#26 with material and box.
- Submit the fill box and leak-detection cable product data with Rev.04.
- Chase RFI#03 and the 19.1 return.
