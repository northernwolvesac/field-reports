Basis and assumptions:
- Pricing per Proposal Standards: $60/hr, OH 20% + misc 2% on base, 8.875% tax on material; totals rounded to $100.
- Quantities from schedules (M6000/M6001) and plans. 1st floor HW piping measured from the vector linework of
  M2014/M2015/M2016 (black 1.2 pt lines, 7,500 ft total incl. HWS+HWR), ~65% taken as HW piping
  (rest is equipment outlines / other): 3,400 lf 1/2"-1" + 1,500 lf 1-1/4"-2-1/2".
- Basement piping sheets M2001/M2003 show no boiler room, mains or glycol piping: allowances 400 lf boiler room,
  1,200 lf mains/risers, 900 lf glycol to FCUs, 400 lf ramp mains, 150 lf pool HX.
- Unit prices are budget numbers (boiler $68k ea, FSD $650 ea, FCU $2,800 ea, etc.), no vendor quotes.
- FSD count = one per apartment (283 = 126 IL + 157 AL), per the M2023 note.
- Not included: EC, fire alarm and GC credits; BMS (needs ABM breakdown); in-slab snow melt tubing.
- Deducts use the same markups as adds (full bid price of the deleted work).
