# Draft response to BC0006 (Clearstory / email to James McMullin, Mark Vetterlein) - not sent

Subject: 11010174 Snowflake - BC0006 Labor and supervision overtime - Northern Wolves response

James, Mark,

Northern Wolves rejects BC0006 (48 hrs labor OT and 40 hrs supervision OT "to date") in full, for the following
reasons.

1. **No substantiation.** BC0006 has no dates, names, hours by day, rates, or description of any work performed.
   Under Subcontract section 1.16, Structure Tone may charge only for labor used to complete or correct Northern
   Wolves' Work. No such work has been identified.

2. **Supervision.** Structure Tone's supervision is part of Structure Tone's general conditions. The Subcontract does
   not provide for a subcontractor to pay for Structure Tone's supervision.

3. **Northern Wolves met section 1.7 at no cost to Structure Tone.** From July through September our crews worked
   overtime and every Saturday, at no cost to Structure Tone. This included 7/11 at Structure Tone's request and the additional sheet metal crews on
   8/1, 8/8, 8/15 and 8/22. Our 7/21 email to the Structure Tone team set out the timeline and confirmed that we were
   absorbing the delays "through overtime, Saturday work, and fabricating in parallel with unfinalized approvals".

4. **Structure Tone's premium days were called for building conditions affecting all trades.**
   - On 7/28 Structure Tone asked Newport, Five Borough and Northern Wolves to price Saturday 8/1 on the 29th floor
     "to help us with the time lost due to noise complaints on that floor during the week".
   - We submitted that premium cost as requested (CR00023, $9,816). Structure Tone voided it without explanation.
   - The 29th floor is directly above an occupied office. We were stopped from installing on 6/29, limited to 6-8 am
     (2 hours per day) until 7/8, and still restricted at the end of July. Deliveries were limited to 6 pm.

5. **The 5/24/26 schedule did not account for these conditions.**
   - Ductwork sketching was set at 3 days starting 6/11, the date the Subcontract was issued.
   - Shop drawing approval was set at 2 weeks, to 6/29.
   - Ductwork installation was set at 6/30 to 7/20 on all floors at once.
   - In fact, all three floors' shop drawings came back Revise & Resubmit (6/22, 6/29, 7/6), and the RFI on the
     exposed main ductwork was open from 6/22 to 7/6. Neither was accounted for.
   - Other items outside our control:
     - temporary piping in the 29th floor main duct route (the same schedule keeps the temp loop until 9/25);
     - architectural awnings not shown on the drawings;
     - the building engineer's revised leak test requirement (all ductwork including low pressure branches up to the
       FPBs, capped and sealed), which we passed the first time on every floor;
     - Bulletin 3 (8/11), which changed ductwork already installed;
     - RFI 3 (6/23 to 8/25) and RFI 6 (answered 8/25);
     - the September pipe riser reroute after the engineer's riser locations proved incorrect. That change order is
       still unapproved.
   - Even so, Structure Tone's 8/14 recap tracked start-ups 10/19, commissioning 11/2 and balancing 11/9, which are the
     same dates as the 5/24/26 schedule.

Please withdraw BC0006.
