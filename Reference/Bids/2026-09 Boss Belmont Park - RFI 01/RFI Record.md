## RFI response format (house)

Cover page: logo, "Request for Information", RFI info block (Project, CM, Engineer, Architect, RFI#, Date, Subject, Drawing No),
contents, reference documents, submitted by / distribution. Then one direct page: numbered items, a solution table, "Sketch" and
"Requested from the Engineer". Attachments stamped "ATTACHMENT A - RFI #NN Rev. N - Northern Wolves AC" with the selected model highlighted.
Keep it direct: proposed solution only, no menu of options.
