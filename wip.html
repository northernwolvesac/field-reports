As of

Revised contract = original contract + change orders (from the latest application's G702 line 3; for contracts without applications: original + approved COs). Billed to date = total completed & stored to date (G702 line 4) on the latest application dated on or before the as-of date; retainage per the application; drafts excluded unless checked. Backlog under contract = revised contract − billed to date. Paid = payments dated on or before the as-of date; A/R = net billed (less retainage) − paid. PM % complete comes from the Workload Planner; earned = revised × PM %, over / (under) billed = billed − earned (blank when the PM has not entered a % complete).

This report is available to the office team only.
