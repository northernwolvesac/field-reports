WIP ReportWork in progress · backlog under contract · costs · over / under billings
As of

Revised contract = original + change orders (G702 line 3 of the latest application; without applications: original + approved COs). Billed to date = G702 line 4 of the latest application dated on or before the as-of date (drafts excluded unless checked); backlog under contract = revised − billed. Est. total cost / cost to date come from Projects → Costs (labor by hand, materials and subs from vendor invoices); % complete = cost to date ÷ est. total cost; earned revenue = revised × % complete; over / (under) billed = billed − earned (positive = billings in excess of costs, negative = costs in excess of billings); est. gross profit = revised − est. total cost; GP to date = earned − cost to date. Projects without cost data fall back to the PM % complete from the Workload Planner (marked "PM %"). Paid = payments dated on or before the as-of date; A/R = net billed (less retainage) − paid.

This report is available to the office team only.
