Estimating RulesCompany rules applied to every estimate — minimum charges, reminders, allowances
Rules run on the Estimating page whenever an estimate is priced. A rule matches estimate lines by a word in the description (and optionally a category) and then either tops the lines up to a minimum charge, adds an allowance (a percent of the matched material or a fixed amount), or shows a reminder to the estimator. Rules never change the catalog prices themselves.
