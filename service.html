Daily e-mail at 8 AM

Every morning at 8 AM (New York time) the people in “Daily e-mail goes to” get one e-mail with: visits to schedule, overdue visits, visits this week, tasks due, proposals to follow up, renewals coming up and active agreements the client has not signed. Nothing is sent on days with nothing on the list.

  1. Open script.google.com with the Google account that should send it → New project.
  2. Paste the code below → Save → choose setup → Run → allow access.
  3. The first e-mail goes out right away; after that every morning at 8.
